## Evidence rules

- No screenshot, source review, green CI badge, or mock-service test alone proves a live integration or production gate.
- Store raw test outputs and deployment identifiers without customer PII, secrets, payment credentials, or live signed URLs.
- A missing external account, contract, credential, legal decision, or owner is a blocker; never replace it with a simulated success.
- Keep staging and production separate. Production deployment is not authorized by a staging pass.
