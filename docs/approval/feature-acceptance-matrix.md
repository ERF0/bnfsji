# Bnafsaji day-one feature acceptance matrix

**Status: draft for founder approval. No row is approved or passed.** This map translates the 61 functional areas in Appendix A of the master prompt into observable outcomes. The founder selected all 61 functional areas for day one, including areas labelled “Later.” Those are product/engineering functions, not 61 product categories. The initial product taxonomy is Clothing and Accessories; admins may propose new categories, which remain inactive until founder approval.

The acceptance statements below are proposals based on the master prompt and recorded founder decisions. They are not authority to add an unapproved provider, publish a legal policy, enable live payments, or deploy. Every `External / decision gate` must be resolved before its feature can be accepted for production. `NOT APPROVED` applies to the entire matrix until the founder signs the PRD and acceptance criteria.

## Recorded scope conflicts and non-negotiable boundaries

- Product taxonomy starts with Clothing and Accessories. Admins may propose new categories; they remain inactive until the founder approves them.
- HyperPay-enabled methods are selected except COD. The master prompt's COD and manual-payment examples are therefore not day-one flows. No payment or seller payout may be marked successful without provider evidence and an approved contract.
- The master prompt includes self-host panels and reverse-proxy products. A Cloudflare-only production architecture would map those functions to Cloudflare-managed deployment/networking; that mapping is not approved yet.
- Founder requested listings/checkout before product-policy approval, then reported that counsel-approved buyer, seller, privacy, returns/refund and payment terms exist and will be provided. Review those terms and confirm they cover live commerce before enabling listings or checkout; keep both disabled until applicable terms and provider/legal gates are verified.
- “No restricted goods” does not override applicable law, HyperPay contract terms or platform provider restrictions. No additional Bnafsaji-specific prohibited-goods list is assumed without founder approval.
